  • Tokyo Station

    Allow about 20 minutes on foot from APA Hotel Kayabacho Hatchobori Ekimae, plus time inside the station. At the Yaesu North entrance by Daimaru, stay outside the ticket gates. The shop moved on Nov 13 and opens at 10:00, after the planned 09:30 station arrival.

Optional souvenir stops in Tokyo

Tokyo Station fits the arrival route. Akihabara and Ginza are separate outings if work and the 18:00 dinner leave enough time. Walking estimates below are one way. See all 15 items from the video.

Tokyo Station · snacks and railway gifts

For Yoku Moku cookie tins, follow First Avenue Tokyo Station, B1, outside the JR gates on the Yaesu side. You can use the hotel directions to the hololive shop in the same shopping area. Yoku Moku shop and floor map.

For a train-melody toy, look for TRAINIART TOKYO in Gransta Tokyo, B1 on the Marunouchi side, inside the conventional JR fare gates. It opens at 10:00, after your planned 09:30 arrival. Use it if you are already inside while it is open. Entering from outside requires a valid ticket for admission or travel. JR lists a related departure-bell capsule series here. Ask what is in stock. TRAINIART floor map · JR melody toy.

Akihabara · GENERAL STORE RAILYARD

This is the shop the video names for the train-melody player. Look outside JR Akihabara Station’s Showa-dori ticket gate. The walk from the Woven meeting building is about 1.5 km / 21 minutes. From the Tokyo hotel it’s about 2.7 km / 39 minutes. Ask which tunes they have before buying. JR store listing and hours.

Ginza · UNIQLO flagship

For the video’s UTme! custom shirts, the Ginza flagship at 6-9-5 Ginza is about 1.9 km / 28 minutes on foot from APA Kayabacho Hatchobori Ekimae. Ask about the print queue before ordering. UNIQLO customization locations.
