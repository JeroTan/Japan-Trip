Optional souvenir stops in Tokyo

Tokyo Station fits the arrival route. Akihabara and Ginza are separate outings if work and the 18:00 dinner leave enough time. Walking estimates below are one way. Browse the shopping guide.

For anime collectibles, choose Akihabara or Nakano Broadway. Okachimachi has three OFF shops close together.

Tokyo Station · snacks and railway gifts

For Yoku Moku cookie tins, follow First Avenue Tokyo Station, B1, outside the JR gates on the Yaesu side. You can use the hotel directions to the hololive shop in the same shopping area. Yoku Moku shop and floor map.

For a train-melody toy, look for TRAINIART TOKYO in Gransta Tokyo, B1 on the Marunouchi side, inside the conventional JR fare gates. It opens at 10:00, after your planned 09:30 arrival. Use it if you are already inside while it is open. Entering from outside requires a valid ticket for admission or travel. JR lists a related departure-bell capsule series here. Ask what is in stock. Nakano Broadway · anime shops and vintage watches

From APA Kayabacho Hatchobori Ekimae, allow about 35 minutes each way by subway and on foot. At Kayabacho take the Tokyo Metro Tozai Line toward Nakano. Choose a train that goes to or through Nakano. At Nakano use the North Exit and walk straight through the covered Sun Mall arcade. Inside Broadway, use the elevators to reach each floor. If you only have a short break before the 18:00 dinner, the Akihabara shops are closer. Building floor maps.

APA Tokyo → Nakano Broadway ↗
Transit map · APA Tokyo → Nakano Broadway

1F · FIREKIDS, units 140–141, has vintage watches in the same building. The shop lists Thursday hours of 11:30–19:30. FIREKIDS branch guide.

2F · HAL Shop for capsule toys and Lashinbang for pre-owned anime goods and figures. Both list 11:00–20:00. Havikoro Toy (ハビコロ玩具, also written Habikoro) has shops on both 2F and 3F. HAL Shop · Lashinbang floor guide · Havikoro shops.

2F–3F · ROBOTROBOT has collectible toys across several counters. Mandarake has specialist shops throughout Broadway, so use its floor guide for manga or the figures you collect. Both operators list 12:00–20:00. ROBOTROBOT floors · Mandarake departments.

4F · One up. Look here for designer toys and Japanese soft-vinyl figures (sofubi). The local shop directory lists 12:00–20:00, closed Wednesdays. Your Tokyo visit is Thursday. One up. shop details · Medicom Toy stockist list.
