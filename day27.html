JINS at AEON Mall Narita

Use the hotel-to-AEON Mall map and guest-shuttle details. BOOKOFF is 1F, unit 183. Take a mall elevator to JINS on 2F, unit 251. Ask when fitted lenses would be ready before ordering. Check your return shuttle before shopping. JINS mall directory.

GU and UNIQLO near AEON Mall Narita

If you already take the hotel guest shuttle to AEON Mall, the two clothing stores are in separate street-side buildings in Wing Tsuchiya. From the mall, GU (124 Wing Tsuchiya) is about 650 m / 9 minutes on foot. Continue about 150 m / 2 minutes to UNIQLO (150 Wing Tsuchiya). Leave time to walk back to Entrance G for the hotel shuttle. From Narita Tobu directly, the road route to UNIQLO is about 6 km. GU address · UNIQLO branch.

Tomisato Inter · three secondhand departments

After fieldwork, consider this stop if the group has a car or you are taking a taxi. From Narita Tobu Hotel Airport the faster road route is about 9 km / 10–15 minutes before traffic and can include a toll. All three shops share 532-41 Nanae, Tomisato and list 10:00–20:00. Choose HARD OFF for used electronics, HOBBY OFF for figures and toys, or OFF HOUSE for used wristwatches, clothing and accessories. HARD OFF · HOBBY OFF · OFF HOUSE watch department.
