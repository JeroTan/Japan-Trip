Optional souvenirs at Narita Terminal 2

Check in, drop bags and check your boarding time first. These shops are in your booked departure terminal. Browse the shopping guide.

The Tomisato reuse shops and hotel map are on the November 27 guide. On departure day, allow time for fieldwork, the airport transfer and bag drop before your 19:00 flight.

Stickers before security, chocolate after

B-SIDE LABEL SKY is on 4F of the T2 main building, before security. If you have time after bag drop, use the elevator up from departures. Directions from Narita Tobu Hotel Airport to T2 · Sticker shop and indoor map.

JAL PLAZA Gate 91 lists ROYCE’ soft chocolate. It is on 3F of the T2 satellite, after international security. Stop only if the satellite is convenient for your assigned gate and boarding time. Ask about a cold pack for the flight. Chocolate shop and indoor map.

UNIQLO before airport security

UNIQLO Terminal 2 is in the main building, 3F before security, on the departures level. The airport lists 07:30–20:30. This is the simplest Narita option for a last clothing purchase if check-in and boarding time allow. Hotel-to-Terminal 2 directions · Official indoor map.
