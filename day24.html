Optional souvenir stops in Nagoya

After hotel check-in, rest and dinner, choose the hotel stop or head to Sakae. The Sakae shops below are local branches of brands in the video. Browse the shopping guide.

7-Eleven by the hotel

Walk about 110 m / 2 minutes from APA Hotel Nagoya Ekimae to the Noritake 1-chome branch at 1-1-7 Noritake. Look here for snack packs, pancake sandwiches or a clear umbrella. For UHA Ninja Meshi gummies, check the candy aisle for 忍者めし. Flavors and stock vary. Branch location.

One Sakae stop for four shops

Use the subway directions and embedded map from the hotel to BOOKOFF. At 3-4-5 Sakae, use the elevators and check the wing on the directory. In SKYLE, UNIQLO is on 5F, GU on 6F and BOOKOFF on 8F. In the adjoining NOVA wing, Zoff eyewear is on 2F and Sakae Loft spans 3F–6F. Head to Loft 5F for pens. Aim to visit these shops before 20:00. Building directory · Loft departments · Zoff Sakae.

For a kuromatsu growing kit, show Loft staff 黒松 栽培セット. Loft lists a black pine kit online. Check branch stock and the Philippine seed rules before buying. For matcha, cross the main intersection to Nagoya Sakae Mitsukoshi and go to the food floor, B1, for the Ippodo counter. For capsule toys, follow the hotel route to #C-pla in the Sakaechika underground mall. Mitsukoshi food directory.

Don Quijote Sakae Main Store

From the hotel-to-BOOKOFF subway route, continue on foot along Otsu-dori, about 250 m / 7 minutes from BOOKOFF. Look for ANESSA sunscreen, KAI nail clippers, kitchenware or luggage. The Sakae branch at 3-17-15 Nishiki lists 24-hour opening. Store details.

GU, UNIQLO and OAO at Nagoya Station

JR Gate Tower, 11F has both GU and UNIQLO. From APA Nagoya it’s about 650 m / 9 minutes on foot to the building, plus time for the elevator. For OAO sneakers, go to the adjacent JR Nagoya Takashimaya main building, 7F, CS Case Study. OAO lists this counter as a stockist. Ask about your size. Gate Tower floor map · OAO stockists.

Panasonic palm shavers near the hotel

Bic Camera Nagoya Station West, 5F is about 400 m / 6 minutes on foot from APA. Look in beauty appliances for LAMDASH Palm In.
