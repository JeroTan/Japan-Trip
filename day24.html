Optional trip from Nagoya

Nishiki Market · Kyoto

Far Allow 75–110 min each way by rail and on foot from APA Nagoya. This is an optional Kyoto side trip with its own rail fare. Walk to Nagoya Station and take a westbound Tokaido Shinkansen toward Shin-Osaka or Hakata that stops at Kyoto. Change to the Karasuma subway line toward Kokusaikaikan, get off at Shijo and walk to the market’s west end at Takakura-dori.

Go for daytime food stalls, pickles and Kyoto specialties. Each shop sets its hours and some close on Wednesdays. Eat at the stall where you bought the food. Your Nov 24 afternoon arrival leaves little shopping time once you factor in the round trip. Market access · Hours and etiquette · Kyoto rail access.

One Sakae stop for four shops
