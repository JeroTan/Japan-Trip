Nagoya hotel to Sakae by subway

  1. APA Nagoya
  2. Nagoya (H08)
  3. Fushimi (H09)
  4. Sakae (H10)

At Nagoya Station: Walk from the hotel to the subway and follow the yellow Higashiyama Line (東山線) signs. Tap your manaca or a supported contactless bank card at its marked reader, or buy a ¥210 paper ticket with yen cash. Step-by-step subway guide · Tickets and tap-to-ride guide. Board toward Fujigaoka (藤が丘). Stay on at Fushimi and get off at Sakae, the second stop. The ride takes about 5 minutes. Allow roughly 20–30 minutes from the hotel including walking and waiting.

For fewer stairs: Follow elevator signs inside Nagoya Station. At Sakae, ask for the inclined elevator into Sakaechika (サカエチカ) near the West Gate. #C-pla is inside the underground mall. For BOOKOFF, follow signs to SKYLE and take the building elevator to floor 8. After 8 PM, BOOKOFF directs visitors through Sakaechika Exit 7.

Going back: At Sakae, take the yellow line toward Takabata (高畑). Get off at Nagoya after two stops and walk back to APA Hotel Nagoya Ekimae.

Nagoya hotel to Sakae by GO Taxi

  1. APA Nagoya entrance
  2. SKYLE, Sakae

Book the ride: In GO, set the pickup pin at a curb by APA Hotel Nagoya Ekimae where a taxi can stop. Enter SKYLE (栄スカイル), 3-4-5 Sakae, Naka-ku as your destination. Check the fare estimate and any pickup or app fees, then tap Request Taxi. Wait on the same side of the road as your pin. If you cannot register for GO, ask the hotel front desk to call a taxi.

At drop-off: Ask for the SKYLE entrance. Take the elevator to BOOKOFF on floor 8, or follow signs to the connected Sakaechika underground mall for #C-pla. Your driver can choose a permitted curb nearby.

Going back: Return to street level, set the pickup pin on your side of the road and enter APA Hotel Nagoya Ekimae as the destination.
